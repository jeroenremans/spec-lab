# S8.9 — Content area and editor fully themed in all 4 themes

**Epic:** EP8 — Theme Switcher  
**Priority:** Must-have

---

**As a** team member who edits and reads specs,  
**I want** the content area and EasyMDE editor to be fully themed,  
**so that** reading and editing are comfortable in all 4 themes without jarring colour mismatches.

**Acceptance intent:** Content background, breadcrumb, page header, markdown body all themed. Edit bar, tag toolbar, EasyMDE surface all themed. Dark themes show dark CodeMirror background — not white.

**Dependencies:** S8.2 (dark CodeMirror override); S8.8.

**Non-goals:** Custom editor fonts; syntax highlighting beyond CodeMirror defaults.
